Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 6, 2026

Who uses this app

Michael Denny Personal MCP is used exclusively by its developer, Michael Denny, with his own Google accounts. No other users are accepted or supported.

What data is accessed

With the developer's explicit consent, the app accesses data in his own Google accounts: Gmail messages and attachments, Google Calendar events and Google Drive files, limited to the scopes granted on the consent screen.

How the data is used

The data is read and modified only to perform tasks the developer requests, for example saving email attachments to his own Drive, updating his own spreadsheets or archiving his own emails. OAuth tokens are stored only on the developer's own computer.

To carry out these tasks, the content involved is processed by the AI assistant the developer uses (Claude, by Anthropic) on his behalf. The data is not sold, not used for advertising and not shared with any other party.

Google API Services User Data Policy

The app's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time from Google Account → Third-party access.
