Michael Denny Personal MCP

A private, single-user tool. It lets an AI assistant running on Michael Denny's own computer access his own Google accounts (Gmail, Google Calendar, Google Drive) through locally hosted Model Context Protocol (MCP) servers, to automate personal tasks such as filing documents and keeping personal spreadsheets up to date.

It is not a public service: it is not offered, distributed or available to anyone else.
